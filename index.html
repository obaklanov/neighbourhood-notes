City quotas follow our population rules, with Halifax included as an exception. Areas are selected by within-city Airbnb listing count where a usable source is connected. Otherwise, we select the highest-population named areas in a connected neighbourhood population dataset, keeping the same city quota. Toronto, Montréal, Vancouver, Ottawa and Mexico City use dated Inside Airbnb snapshots (CC BY 4.0); Calgary, Edmonton and Halifax use Doorstep Analytics public counts (CC BY-SA 4.0). Methods and boundaries differ, so counts should not be compared across providers. Mexico City’s source labels are entire alcaldías (boroughs), not individual colonias. Some other labels are boroughs, wards or districts; city files do not necessarily cover their full metro areas. For 16 Mexican metros without usable Airbnb counts, the fallback uses CONAPO’s estimated 2020 urban-area populations, within the municipalities defined by Metrópolis de México 2020. Source settlement types include colonias, fraccionamientos and pueblos; unnamed areas are excluded.
